# Proposal Template

## Structure (every proposal must follow this)

### Opening (2–3 sentences)
- Show you read the job — reference the client's specific pain
- Mention one relevant credential or comparable result
- No generic "I'm an experienced developer" openers

### The Solution (3–4 bullets)
- Step 1 → Outcome
- Step 2 → Outcome
- Step 3 → Outcome
- Always include: testing, docs, handoff

### Proof (1–2 sentences)
- One specific comparable example (not vague "I've done this before")
- Offer to share sample deliverable if relevant

### Next Step (CTA)
- Offer call OR scope doc — keep it low-friction
- One clear action, not a list of options

---

## Variant: n8n / Workflow jobs

> "I build n8n workflows rather than defaulting to Zapier — that means self-hosted, infinitely customizable, and no execution caps hitting your automation at scale.

> Here's how I'd approach your [job type]:
> - Map your current process → identify every manual step to eliminate
> - Build n8n workflow with error handling, retries, and alerting
> - Test against edge cases, then document for your team

> I've shipped [N] n8n workflows including [brief example]. Happy to share a sample workflow structure.

> Want to jump on a quick call to align on scope?"

---

## Variant: API Integration

> "API integrations are my bread and butter. I've connected [systems] and know the gotchas around [auth/rate limits/data mapping].

> For your [specific integration]:
> - Spec the contract (endpoints, auth, data mapping) — no surprises mid-build
> - Build with error handling, retries, and webhook validation
> - Full test suite + runbook so you can maintain it yourself

> [Proof line].

> Happy to send a more detailed breakdown or jump on a call — your call."

---

## Variant: AI / Chatbot

> "I build AI agents that actually work in production — with proper context handling, fallbacks, and human escalation paths. Not just a demo that breaks after 3 messages.

> For your project:
> - Design conversation flows and fallback paths first
> - Build with context memory, escalation to human, rate limiting
> - End-to-end testing + documentation of all intents and edge cases

> [Proof line].

> Want to align on scope before I quote? 15 minutes saves a lot of back-and-forth."

---

## Red Flag Response Templates

**"What's your best price?"**
> "My pricing reflects deliverables that work reliably in production. Happy to scope a smaller initial phase if budget is tight — often that's a smarter way to start anyway."

**"Can you do it faster?"**
> "I can prioritise this, but rushing usually means bugs later. What's driving the timeline? Sometimes there's a phased approach that gets you the critical part faster."

**Scope creep mid-project:**
> "Let's document the new requirements and I'll quote the additional work separately. Keeps everything clean and protects both of us."
