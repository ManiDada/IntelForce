# Proposal Variants Reference

## Competitive intelligence heuristics

| Proposals Count | Odds | Strategy |
|----------------|------|----------|
| < 5 | Excellent (~20% base) | Submit immediately if fit ≥70% |
| 5–10 | Good | Submit with strong opening |
| 10–20 | Moderate | Only if strong skill match |
| 20–50 | Difficult | Only if clear differentiation |
| 50+ | Very hard | Generally skip unless perfect fit |

**Client quality matters as much as competition count:**
$300K+ client spend with 5 proposals beats $10K client spend with 10 proposals.

---

## Confidence thresholds

- **≥85%** — high confidence, eligible for auto-send gate (once enabled)
- **60–84%** — medium confidence, always flag for review
- **<60%** — do not generate

---

## Opening line patterns by job type

**n8n / workflow:**
"I build n8n rather than defaulting to Zapier — self-hosted, no execution caps, infinitely customizable."

**API integration:**
"API integrations are my bread and butter — I've connected [similar systems] and know exactly where the gotchas are."

**AI/chatbot:**
"I build AI agents that work in production — proper context handling, fallbacks, human escalation. Not just a demo."

**CRM:**
"CRM automation that gets adopted: clean data flows, sensible triggers, and docs your team can actually understand."

**Web scraping:**
"I've done this at scale — anti-bot handling, dynamic content via Playwright, output in whatever format you need."

**Generic strong:**
"I read your spec carefully. Here's how I'd approach it: [specific reference to their actual problem]."

---

## What NOT to write

❌ "I am an experienced developer with X years of experience..."
❌ "I can complete this task efficiently and effectively"
❌ Generic bullet lists that don't reference the client's specific situation
❌ Claiming capabilities you can't cash (voice agents at 30% skill without flagging)
❌ "Looking forward to working with you" before they've hired you
